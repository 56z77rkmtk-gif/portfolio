Oscilliate, 2025

3D animation

3D ArtMotionLoop Animation

Overview

Oscillate is an abstract 3D animation built around repeating movements and subtle visual changes. Elements shift between different states, sometimes controlled, sometimes more organic. The work focuses on motion and how repetition leads to variation.

Background

There's something quietly unsettling about repetition. The more something repeats, the more you start to notice what changes – the small shifts, the tiny deviations that reveal themselves over time. Oscillate sits in that space between order and drift, where patterns don't break but slowly become something else. It's less about movement and more about what movement leaves behind.

Result

The work was inspired by techno and the way visuals are used in that space – how a trace builds up over time, how you move and change with the music but remain caught in a loop, and somehow enjoy exactly that. Each object carries its own energy, its own rhythm, open to be felt differently by whoever is watching.
